Hired in a dual capacity at [GamePoint B.V.](https://www.gamepoint.com), a Dutch online gaming platform: as a **Game Services / Live Operations** specialist and as a **Trainee System Engineer**, supporting both the day-to-day operation of live games and the technical systems behind them. October 2025 to May 2026, Den Haag, The Netherlands.

My primary live-ops focus was **Bingo**, with additional work across **Poker, Royal Dice, and other titles** on the platform, while the system-engineering side covered Linux-based operational support and service stability.

## What I Did

**Live operations & game configuration:** configured and maintained live game environments and game room settings, supported seasonal and live event setups (winter events, summer events, promotional activities), and collaborated with technical and production teams during active operations across multiple game services.

**Technical operations:** used Linux environments to manage configurations and operational tasks when internal tools lacked the required functionality; supported service stability in live production environments.

**SQL & data support:** assisted with database administration tasks and operational queries under senior supervision.

**Automation:** wrote Python scripts to automate repetitive operational workflows, reducing manual effort on routine tasks.

**Incident support:** investigated operational incidents, assisted with resolving live service issues, and maintained workflow continuity during production operations.

## Technologies

Linux · SQL · Python · Game operations tooling · Live service systems

## What I Gained

Live production support for online games is a different operational discipline from development-only roles. Configuration errors have immediate, visible consequences when real players are online. Working in that environment sharpened my technical precision, my ability to prioritise under pressure, and my communication with cross-functional teams, especially when time-to-resolution matters.
