**Horror Environment & Short Cinematic**

I built a dark, horror-themed forest as a personal practice project. The focus was on atmosphere: modeling terrain, foliage and props in Blender, creating PBR textures, and setting up volumetric lighting, particles and subtle ambient SFX to build tension.

I also planned a short cinematic (a few seconds) to practice camera framing, movement and basic color grading: a single shot that communicates mood. The project was an experiment in composition, lighting and optimization so the scene reads well on video.
