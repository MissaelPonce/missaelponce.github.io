An ongoing personal project building a dark, horror-themed forest environment in Blender. The environment is also the set for a short cinematic — a few seconds of camera movement designed to communicate tension and atmosphere.

## What I'm Building

The scene combines modelled terrain, foliage, and props with PBR textures, volumetric lighting, and particle effects. The goal isn't a single hero render — it's a practical environment for practising cinematic framing, camera movement, and basic colour grading on a controlled, purpose-built set.

**Key areas of focus:**
- **Atmosphere** — volumetric fog, particles, and controlled lighting to build dread without overloading the scene
- **Optimisation** — keeping the scene performant enough to iterate and render quickly, even with complex volumetrics active
- **Cinematic composition** — framing and movement that work as video, not just stills

## Status

Environment in active development. Cinematic shot planned for the next stage.
